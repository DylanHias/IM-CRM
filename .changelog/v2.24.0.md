v2.24.0

- New look, part five: Timeline and Follow-Ups are now one Activity page in the sidebar, with a switch between Timeline and Your Follow-Ups
- Both show a list on the left and the selected item on the right, with all its details and buttons to edit, complete or delete it
- The timeline is grouped by day, and follow-ups are grouped into Overdue, Upcoming and Completed
- From an activity you can jump to edit it on the customer, create a follow-up for it or open the customer
- The overdue count now shows on the Activity entry in the sidebar and next to Your Follow-Ups
- Number shortcuts now follow the sidebar exactly, so pages that moved into other places no longer take up a number
