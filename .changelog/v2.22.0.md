v2.22.0

- New look, part three: Customers now shows the list on the left and the selected customer on the right, so you can move between customers without going back and forth
- Search, sorting and active filters sit right above the list; all filters open from the Filters button in a small panel
- New All / Cloud / Non-cloud switch to quickly limit the list to cloud customers
- Use the up and down arrow keys to move through customers; drag the line between the list and the customer to resize the list
- Right-click a customer to open it, add an activity, follow-up or opportunity, favorite it, copy its account number or open it in Dynamics 365
- The customer header shows the email, phone and website (click to copy or open), plus new Open in Dynamics, Follow-up and Log activity buttons
- New Details tab with every company field grouped together; click any field to copy it, and edit the vendor IDs and account team from there
- The Overview tab now shows recent activity, the primary contact and the open opportunities
- Text boxes, dropdowns and buttons are slimmer to match the new look
