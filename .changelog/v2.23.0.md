v2.23.0

- New look, part four: Opportunities is now called Pipeline
- The toolbar shows how many deals are open, their total value and their weighted value
- List view: your deals on the left, the selected deal on the right with its stage bar, key numbers, deal details, people, customer need and latest activity
- New Board view with one column per stage, showing how many deals and how much value sit in each stage
- Close a deal as Won or Lost straight from the deal or the board, without opening the edit form; double-click a deal to edit it
- Right-click a deal to open it, close it, copy its opportunity ID or open it in Dynamics 365
- Search, sorting and filters work like on Customers; Mine has its own button and stages are a filter
- A customer's Opportunities tab now shows the same compact rows, with the number of open deals and their value
