v2.20.0

- New look, part one: fresh colours, a new typeface and a calmer dark mode across the whole app
- The top bar now has a search box in the middle; click it or press Ctrl+K to search
- When changes are waiting to go to Dynamics 365, an orange "pending" label shows next to Online; click it to open Sync
- Sync has moved out of the sidebar into your account menu
- Your account menu opens in the middle of the screen with Sync, Settings, Help, Debug and Shortcuts; your job title is no longer shown
- The sidebar has a New button at the top for logging an activity, adding a follow-up or starting an opportunity
- Favorite and recent customers now open the customer directly instead of expanding into a list of tabs
