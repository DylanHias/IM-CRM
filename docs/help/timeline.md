# Activity

The **Activity** page in the sidebar has two views, switched at the top: **Timeline** and **Your Follow-Ups**. Both show a list on the left and the selected item on the right.

## Timeline

One chronological view of all activities, follow-ups, and opportunities across every customer, grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then the date):

- **Activities** — meetings, calls, visits, and notes, with the customer and the time
- **Follow-ups** — tasks and next actions, with the customer, the due date and whether they're open or done
- **Opportunities** — deals with their stage, value and status

Click an item to see it on the right:

- For an **activity**: its type, status, date, customer, contact, who logged it and the full description. **Edit** opens it on the customer, **Create follow-up** adds a follow-up for the same customer, **Delete** removes it (you'll be asked to confirm) and **Open customer** goes to the customer.
- For a **follow-up**: the same details as in Your Follow-Ups (see below).
- For an **opportunity**: its status, stage, value, vendor and owner, with **Open in Pipeline**.

**Log activity** at the top lets you pick a customer and log a new activity.

## Searching

Use **Search timeline…** above the list to find items by subject or customer name. The list updates as you type.

## Navigation

You can open Activity from:

- The sidebar (Activity entry)
- The command palette (Ctrl+K → "Go to Activity")
- Its number key, following its position in the sidebar
