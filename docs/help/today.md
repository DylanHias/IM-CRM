# Home

Home is your personal overview and the first thing you see when you open the app. It shows what needs your attention today without digging through different sections. You can change which page opens on launch in **Settings → Appearance → Sidebar & navigation → Default landing tab**.

## At the top

The toolbar shows today's date and week number, plus two quick buttons:
- **New follow-up** — pick a customer, then jump straight to the new-follow-up form.
- **Log activity** — pick a customer, then jump straight to the new-activity form.

For a new opportunity, use the **New** button at the top of the sidebar.

## Key figures

One strip with four numbers:
- **Open pipeline** — the annual revenue on your open opportunities, with the change compared to 7 days ago.
- **Lifetime ARR** — the annual revenue on every opportunity you have ever created, no matter how it ended.
- **Win rate · 90 days** — of the deals you closed in the last 90 days, the share you won.
- **Follow-ups due today** — how many follow-ups are due today or overdue, with a red label when some are overdue. **Open tasks** takes you to all your follow-ups.

## Left column

**Due today** — Every open follow-up that is due today or already overdue, oldest first. Overdue ones show in red with how many days late they are. Tick the box to mark one complete (you'll be asked to confirm), or click it to open that customer's Follow-Ups tab.

**Recent activity** — Your latest activities, newest first, with the type, subject, customer and time. Click one to open that customer's Activities tab, or **Open timeline** to see everything.

## Right column

**This week** — Monday to Friday, with one dot per follow-up due that day. Today is highlighted. On weekends it shows the coming week. Click a day to open your follow-ups.

**Deals going quiet** — Open deals with no update in 30 days or more, with their value and how long they've been idle. Click one to open it on the customer's Opportunities tab. (Deals left for a very long time even grow a few cobwebs.)

**Open pipeline by stage** — How the value of your open deals is spread over the stages. **Open pipeline** takes you to the full list.

## Tips

- Press **Ctrl+K** or use the search box in the title bar to find any customer or contact.
- Due today and This week together show what needs attention now and what's coming, so a quick daily look keeps nothing from slipping.
